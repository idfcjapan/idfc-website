International Development Field Camp for Myanmar and Japan Youth Leaders

IDFC International Development Field Camp for Myanmar and Japan Youth Leaders

This is a student conference where young people from Japan and Myanmar live together, discuss ways to address social issues, and share the results with society.

IDFC2027 ↗ About IDFC ↗ Past Activities →

IDFC2026

Learning together, sharing cultures.

In 2026, IDFC returned to an overseas in-person main conference in Bangkok for the first time in six years. The same year, IDFC established its first Myanmar-side organization, opening a new chapter in student-led exchange between the two countries.
