IDFC2023 Main Conference Held

1/21 Osaka, 1/22 Tokyo. Thank you to our speakers and all participants.

The theme for the main conference chosen by us, the IDFC2023 executive committee, is

We want participants to learn about Myanmar’s current situation, the problems it faces, and the relationship between Japan and Myanmar,

And, through that process, they wanted us to consider what actions we could take in the future for ourselves, Japan, and Myanmar.

 

The conference was a very fruitful and meaningful two days: we heard academic talks by university professors, learned about the convictions of people at organizations actively supporting Myanmar, and discussed the relationship between Japan and Myanmar in 2030.

For now, this is just a brief update.

We will report the details of our activities soon.

 

Finally, on behalf of the entire committee, we would like to take this opportunity to thank everyone who helped make the main conference possible.

(Top: Osaka venue; bottom: Tokyo venue)

1. Hear both the academic perspective from university professors and the practical perspective from organizations actively supporting Myanmar!

3. Learn about Myanmar, which is receiving less news coverage!

Recruiting Temporary Committee Members for the IDFC2023 Main Conference (until 12/15)

Hello. We are the Japanese Executive Committee for IDFC2023.

 

We have decided to hold the “IDFC2023 Main Conference” in the winter of the coming year. We are therefore recruiting “temporary executive committee members” who will facilitate the conference on the day and help with preparations beforehand.

Everyone is welcome, from those interested in Myanmar to those who simply want to experience the atmosphere of a student conference 🤗

We look forward to your applications!
