What is IDFC?

International Development Field Camp for Myanmar and Japan Youth Leaders (IDFC) is a voluntary, non-incorporated organization serving as the organizing body of the annual Japan-Myanmar Student Conference. Established in 2014, marking the 60th anniversary of diplomatic relations between Japan and Myanmar, IDFC provides a youth-led platform that connects students from both nations to tackle shared social challenges, build mutual trust, and develop regional solutions.

ORGANIZATION AT A GLANCE

  • Full Name : International Development Field Camp (IDFC)
  • Org Type : Voluntary, Non-Incorporated Student Conference Body
  • Established : 2014 (60th Anniversary of Japan-Myanmar Relations)
  • Core Body : Executive Committee Members (Japan & Myanmar)

Our Vision

"Create an opportunity for exchanging ideas between the youths of Myanmar and Japan, constructing sustainable relationships that continues for mutual future profit."

Our 5 Core Missions

  • Cultural Sharing: Facilitate direct, multi-faceted understanding of each nation's traditional and modern youth cultures to broaden individual mindsets.
  • Mutual Learning: Teach methodology for social problem-solving, exchange knowledge, and empower next-generation youth leaders.
  • Shared Social Concern: Enable delegates to identify common social interests, problem awareness, and mutual goals beyond borders.
  • Collaborative Action: Engage delegates in co-creating innovative solutions to real-world challenges through field research, workshops, and public symposia.
  • Sustainable Network Building: Cultivate long-term human networks across students, alumni, universities, NPOs, and corporate partners.

Core Values

  • Youth-Led Leadership: Driving grassroots initiative and autonomy.
  • Cross-Cultural Respect: Valuing diverse perspectives through open dialogue.
  • Field-Oriented Action: Grounding solutions in direct local observation and interaction.
  • Long-Term Partnership: Building enduring bilateral trust.

IDFC is currently seeking the following two types of sponsors.

 

Companies

 

　 We at IDFC International Development Field Camp for Myanmar and Japan Youth Leaders work toward the main conference held every February under the vision that “young people in Japan and Myanmar create their own opportunities for exchange and build relationships for the future.” At the main conference, we provide a setting where 30 students from the two countries discuss shared social issues and deepen mutual understanding.

IDFC’s activities are funded mainly by grants from foundations and participation fees (Japan side: ¥95,000; Myanmar side: ¥25,000). However, grants from foundations present obstacles to continuity, such as restrictions on consecutive awards. As for participation fees, we aim to reduce the fee for participants from Myanmar because we want to open the door more widely to students there, where disparities between rich and poor are especially pronounced.

For IDFC to remain a platform for ambitious young people from Japan and Myanmar, support from companies is essential. We therefore ask whether you would be able to provide financial assistance for organizing and running the conference. We would also greatly appreciate sponsorship in the form of in-kind support.

If you are considering supporting us, we would appreciate hearing from you. Please contact us through the inquiry page.

 

 

Individual Supporters

　 The IDFC International Development Field Camp for Myanmar and Japan Youth Leaders is a student organization that aims to develop globally minded people and leadership through discussions of social issues shared by Japan and Myanmar, and through cultural exchange. Students handle everything from planning the main conference to fundraising, and we continue to serve as a platform that contributes to society in both countries.

A stable financial foundation is essential for us to sustain our activities. We would therefore appreciate your financial support for holding and running the conference. All members of the IDFC Executive Committee sincerely look forward to your support.

We welcome contributions from individual supporters starting at ¥10,000 per unit. If you are considering supporting us, we would appreciate hearing from you.

If you are considering supporting us, we would appreciate hearing from you. Please contact us through the inquiry page.
